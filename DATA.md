# Data Sources

This document describes the datasets required to replicate the analysis. Raw data files are not included in this repository due to size constraints; they should be placed in `data/raw/` following the structure below.

## 1. Kelp Canopy and Biomass (Bell et al. 2023)

- **Description**: Quarterly NetCDF files of kelp biomass in the canopy from Landsat 5, 7, and 8, since 1984.
- **Citation**: Bell, T.W. et al. (2023). "Kelpwatch: A new visualization and analysis tool to explore kelp canopy dynamics." *PLOS ONE* 18(3): e0271477.
- **URL**: https://sbclter.msi.ucsb.edu/data/catalog/package/?package=knb-lter-sbc.74 (also on EDI: https://portal.edirepository.org/nis/mapbrowse?scope=knb-lter-sbc&identifier=74&revision=newest)
- **File**: `data/raw/knb-lter-sbc.74.16/LandsatKelpBiomass_2022_Q1_withmetadata.nc`
- **Access**: Public, no registration required.
- **Variables used**: `biomass` (kg), `area` (m²), `latitude`, `longitude`, `time`.

## 2. AIS Vessel Tracking Data (U.S. Coast Guard / MarineCadastre)

- **Description**: Automatic Identification System vessel positions, aggregated to hexagon--month level (total distance traveled and vessel count) by the co-author.
- **Citation**: "AIS data accessed via MarineCadastre.gov, jointly maintained by the U.S. BOEM and NOAA." (paper footnote)
- **URL**: https://marinecadastre.gov/ais/
- **File**: `data/raw/exposure_shipping.csv`
- **Access**: Public. Bulk data available from 2009 onward. Free account required for AccessAIS tool.
- **Note**: AIS data begins January 2009; no pre-2009 vessel tracking is available.

## 3. ERA5 Reanalysis: Significant Wave Height

- **Description**: ERA5 monthly averaged significant wave height on single levels, aggregated to hexagon--quarter level.
- **Citation**: Hersbach, H. et al. (2023). ERA5 hourly data on single levels from 1940 to present. Copernicus Climate Change Service, Climate Data Store.
- **URL**: https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/datasets/reanalysis-era5-single-levels-monthly-means
- **Files**: `data/raw/swh_mean_official.csv` (2005--2014, pre-aggregated by co-author); `data/raw/era5_swh_2015_2016_monthly.nc` (2015--2016 extension, downloaded via `code/download_swh_2015_2016.py`).
- **Access**: Free CDS account and API key (`~/.cdsapirc`) required.
- **Variable**: `significant_height_of_combined_wind_waves_and_swell`. Bounding box: [43, -126, 26, -113].

## 4. ERA5 Reanalysis: Precipitation

- **Description**: ERA5 monthly averaged precipitation, aggregated to hexagon--quarter level by co-author.
- **Citation**: Same as above (Hersbach et al. 2023).
- **URL**: Same as above.
- **File**: `data/raw/precipitation_mean_official.csv`
- **Access**: Same as above.

## 5. Sea Surface Temperature (MODIS)

- **Description**: MODIS monthly SST composites, aggregated to hexagon--quarter level by co-author.
- **File**: `data/raw/sst_mean_official.csv`
- **Access**: Public.
- **Note**: Exact MODIS product ID not documented in codebase; file was pre-processed by co-author.

## 6. Cumulative Human Impact Index (Halpern et al. 2009)

- **Description**: Cumulative human impacts to California Current marine ecosystems, combining 25 anthropogenic stressors at ~1 km² resolution.
- **Citation**: Halpern, B.S. et al. (2009). "Mapping cumulative human impacts to California Current marine ecosystems." *Conservation Letters* 2(3): 138--148.
- **URL**: https://databasin.org/datasets/42131dc0bcf64f829611520d4a427ad2/
- **File**: `data/raw/full_model.tif`
- **Access**: Public via Data Basin (free account required).

## 7. NBIC Vessel Arrivals Database

- **Description**: National Ballast Information Clearinghouse records of commercial vessel arrivals at U.S. ports. Used for financial crisis robustness check.
- **URL**: https://nbic.si.edu/database/
- **File**: `data/raw/nbic/nbic_vessel_arrivals.csv`
- **Access**: Requires data use agreement. Maintained by Smithsonian Environmental Research Center and U.S. Coast Guard.
- **Variables used**: Overseas vessel arrivals at Los Angeles, Long Beach, San Diego, and Port Hueneme (2004--2023).

## 8. Southern California Bight 2008 Sediment Chemistry

- **Description**: Sediment PAH concentrations from the Bight 2008 Regional Monitoring Program. Used for illustrative appendix figure only (not in econometric pipeline).
- **Citation**: Schiff, K., J. Gosselin, and D. Diehl (2011). "Southern California Bight 2008 Regional Monitoring Program: Volume IV Sediment Chemistry." SCCWRP Technical Report 628.
- **URL**: https://www.sccwrp.org/about/research-areas/regional-monitoring/southern-california-bight-regional-monitoring-program/bight-program-data-portal/
- **Access**: Available through SCCWRP Bight Program Data Portal.

## 9. ECA 10 km Hexagonal Grid

- **Description**: Hexagonal grid covering the 2009 ECA regulated zone and 2011 boundary modification area. Project-generated by co-author.
- **File**: `data/raw/ECA_10km.gpkg` (1,719 hexagons)
- **Access**: Provided with replication package.

## 10. Kelp Equivalence Covariates

- **Description**: Lookup table linking kelp hexagon IDs to ECA hexagon IDs. Project-generated by co-author.
- **File**: `data/raw/kelp_equivalence_covariates.gpkg`
- **Access**: Provided with replication package.
